## What I'm Building

### 🚀 [Nexora](https://github.com/muhammadfisabilillah/Nexora)

**AI-powered career intelligence platform**

Nexora is my main product project, focused on helping students and job seekers create better resumes and prepare for their careers.

**Built with:**

`Next.js` `React` `TypeScript` `Prisma` `PostgreSQL` `Supabase` `Tailwind CSS` `Docker`

* AI-powered resume analysis
* Resume builder & editor
* Profile, experience, education, skills & projects management
* Authentication & email verification
* Resume preview
* Persistent AI analysis
* Career-focused workflow

### 🛡️ [AI Verify](https://github.com/muhammadfisabilillah/ai-verify)

**Risk-adaptive verification for AI-generated software**

AI Verify is an open-source experiment exploring a simple idea:

> **The more risky the change, the stronger the verification should be.**

Instead of running every check for every change, AI Verify analyzes what changed, estimates the risk, and selects appropriate verification steps.

**Built with:**

`TypeScript` `Node.js` `Git` `Vitest`

Published as an npm package and designed to work with different software projects rather than being tied to a single framework.
